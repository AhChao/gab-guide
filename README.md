# Gab Guide - English Small Talk Analyzer

Gab Guide is your personal AI-powered English coach designed to help you master the art of small talk. Simply paste your conversation transcripts, and get instant feedback on your grammar, naturalness, and flow.

[Try it!](https://gab-guide.omnivorouscat.com/)
NOTE: open it in your browser rather than inside a chat app. Your conversations, saved expressions and check-ins are stored in your browser, so a chat app's built-in viewer will not keep them for next time.

## ✨ Key Features

### Review
- **Message Analysis**: Click any of your messages for grammar and naturalness feedback with more natural alternatives, or analyze the whole conversation at once.
- **Conversation Summaries**: An overview of clarity and flow across the chat, plus vocabulary suggestions.
- **Save to Practice**: Keep the expressions worth learning; export a report as PDF or TXT.

### Practice
- **Daily Review**: Saved expressions come back on a schedule until you can say them without a hint.
- **Hear It Said**: Real clips of native speakers and a sample recording for each expression, and you can record yourself to compare.
- **Practice Targets**: Topic Idea opens a ChatGPT conversation that quietly steers toward your saved expressions; paste it back to see which ones you used on your own.

### Progress
- **Check-ins**: Every 2 to 4 weeks, the same set of questions in ChatGPT, pasted back with word counts and other plain numbers.
- **Compare**: Two check-ins side by side, with quotes from both as evidence instead of scores.
- **Backup**: Export everything to a file and import it on another device.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Access the App**: Open the deployed Gab Guide URL.
2. **Set your API Key**: Click the settings icon and enter your Gemini API key (the key is only stored in your browser).
3. **Analyze**: Paste a conversation transcript and start improving your "gab"!

The native speaker clips and sample recordings in Practice work without an API key.
